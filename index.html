Biography

Khanh Nguyen is a postdoctoral researcher at the National Research Laboratory for Bioinspired Systems, Konkuk University (KU), South Korea. His research is supported by the Korea Research Institute for Defense Technology Planning and Advancement (KRIT) through the Defense Acquisition Program Administration (DAPA), and the National Research Foundation of Korea (NRF). He earned his Bachelor of Engineering from Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology (HCMUT), Vietnam, where he completed the selective five-year Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering program PFIEV, accredited by the CTI and recognized with the EUR-ACE Master's label.

He is a 2026 recipient of the BK21 Fellowship for postdoctoral scholars, awarded by the Korean government. His honors include the Outstanding Dissertation Award from the Korean Society of Mechanical Engineers, the Excellent Academic Award from the College of Engineering at Konkuk University, and Best Conference Paper Awards from the International Conference on Intelligent Unmanned Systems (2022, 2025) and the Korean Society for Aeronautical and Space Sciences (2024). Earlier distinctions include Excellent Student and Quintessential Student awards from HCMUT, the Quintessential Student Distinction from Tran Phu High School, and a Top 1% Nationwide ranking in the Vietnam National University Entrance Examination (2013).

Nguyen's research focuses on insect-like flapping-wing robots and tail-beat underwater robotic fish. He has published nine peer-reviewed journal articles, including seven in Q1 journals (Top 10%), and is preparing an additional co-first-authored manuscript as co-corresponding author.

Beyond research, Nguyen leads student groups at HCMUT focused on bioinspired locomotion. He helps students explore motion principles in nature through simulation and apply them to unmanned vehicle systems, while welcoming collaboration in related fields.
